# Ethiopia Macroeconomic Shock Platform

🚧 Work in progress.

An interactive dashboard estimating how global oil and fertilizer price shocks,
together with the exchange rate, pass through into consumer price inflation in Ethiopia,
using a Vector Autoregression (VAR) model.

## Policy context

This project empirically examines the commodity and exchange-rate channels highlighted in:

N'Sonde, R. O., Assah, F., & Mengistu, M. T. (2026). *Statement by the Executive Director
on the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia.* In *The Federal Democratic Republic of
Ethiopia: Fourth Review Under the Extended Credit Facility Arrangement* (IMF Country Report
No. 26/20). International Monetary Fund. https://doi.org/10.5089/9798229035569.002
